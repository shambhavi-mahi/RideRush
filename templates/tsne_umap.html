About t-SNE

t-SNE (t-distributed Stochastic Neighbor Embedding) is a non-linear dimensionality reduction technique well-suited for visualizing high-dimensional data in 2D or 3D. It models each high-dimensional object by a two-dimensional point such that similar objects are modeled by nearby points and dissimilar objects are modeled by distant points with high probability.

Perplexity controls the balance between local and global structure. Low perplexity (5–10) focuses on local neighborhood structure. High perplexity (30–50) captures more global structure. The optimal value typically lies between 5 and 50.

Note: t-SNE is non-deterministic. For performance, the dataset is subsampled to 800 records for this visualization.
