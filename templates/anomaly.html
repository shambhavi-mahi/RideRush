About the Methods

Isolation Forest isolates anomalies by randomly selecting a feature and a split value between the max and min of that feature. Since anomalies are few and different, they require fewer splits to isolate, resulting in shorter paths in the isolation tree.

Local Outlier Factor (LOF) computes the local density deviation of a data point with respect to its neighbors. Points with substantially lower density than their neighbors are considered outliers. LOF is effective at detecting local anomalies in non-uniform data distributions.
